Step 1 of 3 · Logo exploration

Five logo concepts for Kaagapay

Each concept uses the website's existing colours (sage, warm clay and cream) and its heading typeface, Source Serif 4, so whichever one is chosen will fit the current site. Every concept is shown large, as a logo, at favicon size and on a dark background, then scored against the brand brief.

1

The concepts

The brief: warm and welcoming, modern and premium, trustworthy, and easy to recognise for older clients and their families.

1. Magkaagapay

★ Recommended

"Side by side": a companion and a senior who together form one heart.

The companion (sage) and the senior (clay) lean toward each other, and their bodies join into a heart. It says exactly what kaagapay means, "the one who walks beside you", without needing words. It also grows out of the heart already used in the website header, so the site stays recognisable.

Strengths

  • Tells the story at a glance: people, care, togetherness
  • Natural next step from the current heart mark
  • Still reads as a heart at 16px
  • Two colours give a warm, human feel

Watch-outs

  • People-and-heart marks are popular in care brands; the two-tone split and gap make this one distinctive
  • Needs the small-size version under 24px
Brief score28 / 30

2. Kapit-Bisig

"Arm in arm": two linked rings, woven over and under.

Two rings hook together like linked arms, a symbol of support and staying close. It is geometric, simple and holds up well when printed or embroidered on uniforms.

Strengths

  • Clean, modern and easy to reproduce
  • Good on uniforms, stamps and signage

Watch-outs

  • Easily read as wedding rings, a bank or a tech company
  • Abstract: does not show people or care
  • The weave disappears at 16px
Brief score21 / 30

3. Tahanan

"Home": a roof sheltering a heart.

A soft roofline protects a warm heart: care and safety, like family. It is very easy to understand for every age group.

Strengths

  • Instantly understood, even by first-time visitors
  • Strong at small sizes

Watch-outs

  • Suggests in-home nursing or real estate, but Kaagapay's companions go out with clients (errands, walks, appointments)
  • Used by many home-care agencies, so harder to stand out
Brief score23 / 30

4. Dahon

"Leaves": a tall leaf growing beside a smaller one, from the same stem.

A strong leaf stands beside a smaller, older one: gentle support and a life still growing. It feels calm and premium, close in mood to a wellness brand.

Strengths

  • The most elegant and premium of the five
  • Pairs naturally with the sage palette

Watch-outs

  • Could be mistaken for a spa, organic food or a pharmacy
  • Doesn't show people, so companionship has to be explained

5. K-Puso

"K with a heart": a rounded K monogram that holds a heart.

A friendly, rounded letter K with a small heart held between its arms. It works as a monogram on uniforms, ID lanyards and app icons.

Strengths

  • Builds recognition of the name's first letter
  • Bold, simple monogram

Watch-outs

  • Letter monograms say little about the service
  • The heart is lost at 16px
  • Feels more corporate than warm
Brief score19 / 30

Recommendation

Concept 1, Magkaagapay

It is the only concept that shows the service itself: a person walking beside an older person, with care at the centre. Families get it at a glance, older clients see people and a heart rather than an abstract symbol, and it develops the heart already in the website header, so returning visitors still recognise Kaagapay.
