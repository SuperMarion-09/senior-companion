Kaagapay brand guidelines

Typography

Two free typefaces (SIL Open Font License), both already used on the website. A friendly serif for headings, and a sans serif designed for low-vision readers for everything else.

Headings and wordmark
Aa Gg

Source Serif 4

SemiBold 600 and Bold 700. Warm and trustworthy, like a well-made letter. Use for headings, quotes and the tagline.

ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWXYZ abcdefghijklmnopqrstuvwxyz 0123456789 ñ

Body text and interface
Aa Gg

Atkinson Hyperlegible

Regular 400 and Bold 700. Made by the Braille Institute so letters like I, l and 1 never look alike. Use for paragraphs, buttons, forms and labels.

ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWXYZ abcdefghijklmnopqrstuvwxyz 0123456789 ñ

Keep it large

Body text is never smaller than 18px on screens or 12pt in print. Nothing smaller than 16px.

Sentence case

"Book a companion", not "BOOK A COMPANION". Capitals only for short labels like "CONFIRMED".

Fallbacks

Where the fonts can't load (most email apps): Georgia for headings, Arial for text.

Logo spacing and minimum sizes

Give the logo room to breathe, and never show it so small that the two figures blur together.

xxxx

Clear space

Keep an empty space of at least x on every side of the logo, where x is the height of the companion's head plus the gap below it (about a quarter of the mark's height). No text, edges or other logos may enter it.

x = head + gap. With the logo 40px tall, as in the website header, x is about 11px.

Correct and incorrect logo use

Use the supplied files exactly as they are. If a background makes the logo hard to read, change the logo version, not the logo.

Do

✓Full colour on cream or white
✓Reversed logo on Night Sage
✓White logo on Sage or Clay
✓White logo on a darkened photo

Don't

✕Don't stretch or squash it
✕Don't change the colours
✕Don't rotate or tilt it
✕Don't add shadows, glows or outlines
✕Don't use full colour on dark backgrounds
✕Don't place it on busy patterns or photos
Kaagapay
✕Don't retype the name, change the font or rearrange the parts
✕Don't flip the mark or resize the mark and name separately
6

Iconography and illustration style

Simple, friendly and never clinical. Icons and illustrations help people find their way; they always come with a clear text label.

Icons

  • Line icons on a 24 × 24 grid with a 2px stroke and rounded ends and corners: the same set as the website (Icon.js).
  • Shown at 20–32px, in Sage, Clay or the text colour. Never smaller than 20px.
  • Always next to a word. An icon alone can be misread, especially by older users.
  • Everyday objects, not medical ones. Calendars, bags, footprints and hands; no pills, syringes or crosses, because Kaagapay is non-medical.
  • For feature tiles, place the icon in a rounded square of Soft Sage or Clay Blush.

Illustration

The homepage illustration sets the style for any new drawings.

  • Flat shapes, rounded forms, no outlines and no gradients.
  • Brand colours plus warm skin tones that reflect Filipino families.
  • Show seniors as active and capable: walking, shopping, laughing, choosing. The companion stands beside them, never over them.
  • Everyday Filipino settings: palengke, park, church, barangay hall, merienda at home.
  • Avoid hospital scenes, wheelchairs as the main subject, sad or lonely moods, and clip-art.
  • Photos: real Kaagapay companions and clients, only with written consent, in natural light.
